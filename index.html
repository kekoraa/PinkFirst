Our Mission

Care Begins with Awareness

Our mission is to empower women to lead informed, healthy lives by advocating for comprehensive women's health awareness. We provide accessible resources, knowledge, and community support — focusing on preventative care, holistic well-being, and personal empowerment. Through education and outreach, we equip every woman with the tools to make proactive health decisions and foster a supportive environment where women can share their stories, gain strength, and inspire one another.

Values

What We Stand For

Empowerment through Knowledge

We believe that informed women are empowered women. By providing accessible, accurate information on women's health and breast cancer, we want to enable individuals to make proactive choices for their well-being.

Inclusivity and Accessibility

Health information should be accessible to all, regardless of age, background, or location. We want to strive to create resources and programs that reach women who might otherwise lack access, including college-aged women and those in underserved or overseas communities.

Community Support and Compassion

Our strength lies in the power of community. We want to foster a supportive environment where women can connect, share experiences, and uplift each other in their health journeys, from prevention to recovery.

Holistic Health and Wellness

True wellness encompasses both body and mind. We want to advocate for a balanced approach to health that includes mental well-being, self-care, and self-compassion alongside physical health.

Advocacy and Outreach

We are committed to raising awareness and advocating for women's health on a local, national, and global scale. Through outreach, partnerships, and education, we want to work to dismantle barriers and bring critical health knowledge to as many women as possible.

Empathy and Respect for Individual Journeys

Every woman's health journey is unique, and we honor each individual's experiences and needs. We want to approach all interactions with empathy, respect, and a commitment to celebrating diversity in every form.

Our Story

Born from Personal Experience, Built for Every Woman

Pink First was founded in 2025 by Nitin Ponduri and Chirayu Pradhan and established as a registered nonprofit organization. The idea was deeply personal — Nitin's mother was diagnosed with breast cancer, and that experience gave him a firsthand understanding of how overwhelming and under-resourced a woman's health journey can be. He knew something had to change.

Together, Nitin and Chirayu created Pink First with a clear purpose: to promote breast cancer awareness, provide essential resources to women in need, and empower communities through education and outreach. Pink First is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes, operating in accordance with Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Our name says it all — we put women first. Their health, their dignity, and their futures are at the center of everything we do.

Our Mission

To improve the health and well-being of women by providing essential resources, education, and community support that promote holistic wellness, preventative care, and long-term empowerment — because every woman deserves access to dignified, informed healthcare.

Our Vision

A world where every woman, regardless of zip code, income, or circumstance, has access to the care, knowledge, and support she needs to live a healthy, fulfilling life. We envision communities where women's health is prioritized at every stage — where preventative care is the norm, awareness is widespread, and no woman faces her health journey alone.

Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Last updated: January 1, 2024

Pink First is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe that health information and community resources should be available to all women, and our website reflects that commitment.

Our Commitment

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

Accessibility Features on This Site

We have implemented the following features to improve accessibility:

  • Keyboard navigation: All pages and interactive elements can be navigated using a keyboard alone
  • Skip link: A "Skip to main content" link is available at the top of every page for screen reader and keyboard users
  • Focus indicators: Visible focus rings are displayed on all interactive elements
  • ARIA labels: Buttons and icon-only elements include descriptive labels for assistive technologies
  • Alt text: All meaningful images include descriptive alternative text
  • Color contrast: Text and interactive elements meet or exceed the WCAG AA minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1
  • Responsive design: The site is fully usable on mobile devices and adjusts to different screen sizes and zoom levels
  • Reduced motion: Animations and transitions are minimized or disabled for users who have enabled the "reduce motion" preference on their device
  • Semantic HTML: The site uses proper heading structure, landmark regions, and semantic elements to support screen reader navigation

Assistive Technologies Supported

This website is designed to be compatible with the following assistive technologies:

  • Screen readers (including NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver, and TalkBack)
  • Keyboard-only navigation
  • Browser zoom up to 200% without loss of content or functionality
  • High contrast display modes

Known Limitations

While we work hard to ensure accessibility, some content may not yet fully meet all guidelines. We are actively working to identify and address these gaps. Areas we are continuing to improve include:

  • Ensuring all third-party embedded content meets accessibility standards
  • Providing captions or transcripts for any future video content
